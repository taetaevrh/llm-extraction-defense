## Scope, completed evidence and immediate decision

Study **task-specific behavioral extraction**: an attacker submits banking-intent text to a fixed hosted Teacher and uses its released label strings to adapt a smaller pretrained model. Labels are text outputs; the simulated attacker cannot inspect Teacher parameters, logits or hidden states. This is not recovery of all LLM capabilities, exact parameters, or memorized training records. Detection identifies observable suspicious traffic; prevention/degradation reduces useful copying under a stated budget; ownership verification identifies a suspect copy. These are separate outcomes. ## Research gap to test

Existing traffic detectors can exploit query-distribution shifts, and existing response defenses may trade legitimate utility for protection. The local unanswered question is whether any useful signal or allocation advantage survives **matched task/source/label traffic, repeated legitimate queries and adaptive attackers**, within a500-attempt extraction study. It is possible that no reliable separation exists. If benign and attacker observable histories have the same joint distribution (including session lengths, timing, texts and released labels), every detector using only those observations has equal alarm probability for both populations. Malicious intent alone cannot create a statistical signal. Equal marginal query distributions do not exclude sequential differences; test the complete observable-history assumption explicitly.

All algorithms below are **unimplemented proposals**. Freeze any future encoder revision/license, similarity rule, calibration cohorts, parameters, tests and rejection rule before feasibility inference. Ground truth may label evaluation cohorts, never calibrate a detector to attack examples or train the attacker.

## H1: intent-conditioned recurrence residuals

**Hypothesis:** accounting for benign within-intent repetition reduces false alarms under legitimate label-mixture shifts without removing a detectable excess recurrence signal from iterative extraction. This is the strongest candidate for a **falsifiable feasibility study**, not the strongest proven defense.

Algorithm: maintain per-account/session normalized query embeddings and the ordinary released predicted intent (an invalid output gets its own stratum). A frozen small encoder runs on CPU or in a separate GPU phase; no Teacher internals or extra inference calls. In each recent window of32,64,128 queries, for each intent with at least four rows, compute the fraction of within-intent pairs whose cosine similarity exceeds that intent's benign-reference99th-percentile pair cutoff. Fit expected pair fractions and scale from benign-only reference windows **matched to observed per-intent counts**. Define a standardized excess-recurrence residual per supported intent; use equal weights over supported intents, not raw class frequency. Sparse windows abstain from an alarm and record coverage/delay. The session score is the maximum residual aggregate over the three window lengths and all declared look times up to500 queries. Calibrate a single threshold on independent benign calibration-session **maxima**, targeting1% per-session FPR. This calibrates multiple looks jointly, not one threshold reused as if windows were independent. Freeze handling of zero variance (positive fixed floor from development) and insufficient benign match support (no decision), including the resulting loss of detection coverage.

Closest priors: PRADA already conditions distances on predicted class, SEAT already counts similar pairs, and semantic MMD already tests traffic against benign references; see [primary-method review](task04_related_work.md). The proposed difference is the **count-matched benign conditional null plus joint session-maximum calibration under changing intent mixtures**, rather than Gaussian-distance fit, raw pair count or unconditional discrepancy. Conditional calibration is itself established statistical practice; this combination has no established novelty. Additional output-label information makes it a stronger defender than a query-only comparator; disclose that advantage and include a label-aware simple baseline.

Assumptions/failures: stable benign conditional semantics, enough legitimate calibration sessions per intent, meaningful session identity, repeated extraction variants that remain close in embedding space. Benign support conversations may repeat heavily; independent pool stealing may be indistinguishable; unknown classes, calibration drift, paraphrases, noisy Teacher labels, distributed accounts and history resets may erase signal. Do not interpret alarms as proof of intent.

Ablations: remove intent conditioning; keep conditioning but remove count matching; use one window with the same session-level calibration; replace recurrence by nearest-distance or simple class-frequency scores. Compare query-only MMD, semantic adaptations of PRADA/SEAT with their deviations disclosed, label-aware nearest-neighbor monitoring, and a trivial query-count alarm. Use equal false-positive constraints and the same embedding model; do not attribute gains from a better encoder to the score.

Proposed rejection criteria for research review: held-out benign-session FPR exceeds its prespecified1% target (with its binomial uncertainty and finite resolution reported), or the paired95% interval for TPR improvement over the strongest matched comparator includes no positive gain and the point improvement is <5 percentage points under class-mixture-shift traffic. Reject practical usefulness if benefits vanish under source/length matching, benign-repetition controls, or a query-filtering/paraphrasing attacker. An inconclusive small sample is not proof of no signal. Maximum latency/CPU-memory limits must be agreed and frozen from development measurements; no claims that an encoder fits this GPU have been measured here.

Feasibility: at most128 embeddings per active session, CPU pairwise operations and a compact frozen encoder; encode/store benign reference embeddings once. This avoids simultaneous large-model residency. Multi-session state scales with session count; bound memory and report it. No extra encoder was downloaded or trained in Task04.

## H2: suspicion-gated per-region answer credits

**Hypothesis:** distributing a limited response allowance across semantic regions can reduce repeated local supervision more than uniform random denial at the same attempted budget and legitimate coverage, conditional on a usable traffic signal.

Algorithm proposal: cluster only benign development embeddings into32 fixed semantic regions with seed17; freeze centroids and nearest-centroid assignment. Initialize eight answer credits per region per authenticated session; maintain region counts from the session's start. Until a separately calibrated suspicion flag, answer normally. After the flag, return the undefended Teacher's ordinary label only when that region has remaining credit; otherwise return one explicit refusal token, never a false label. Decrement credit only on a released valid label, but count every attempted call including refusal/invalid/error against the attack budget. No automatic refill during a500-attempt session; reset behavior and identity lifetime are explicit assumptions. Treat small-region capacity/imbalance and defaults as development-only proposals requiring review, not tuned final values.

Closest priors are stateful monitoring, ordinary quotas, output denial and coverage-aware defenses; CAP's full method remains inaccessible (403), so the precise novelty comparison is **blocked**. Relative to random denial or a global quota, the testable change is per-region allocation after the same suspicion signal, retaining accurate released answers. It is not Knowledge Trap's response-guided exploration, and it is not gradient-based posterior poisoning. A combination of these existing ingredients cannot be claimed new without a defensible further distinction and complete prior comparison.

Failure assumptions: correlated benign support sessions may exhaust regions, attackers can spread among regions/accounts or avoid the suspicion trigger, and credits can encourage diverse informative queries. If the attacker samples natural input traffic indistinguishably, targeted protection is unavailable. Stable identity is essential; do not assume account splitting is costly without evidence. Abstention is a real legitimate utility loss even if answered-query accuracy is unchanged.

Ablations/comparators: uniform random denial, global per-session query cap, ungated per-region credits, and the same suspicion detector followed by uniform denial. Match **actual** denial rate on attack and benign cohorts separately as well as aggregate coverage; equal pooled rates can hide disparate harm. Rate parameters may be calibrated on development only; matched retrospective masks are explicitly oracle-rate controls, not deployable policies. Evaluate no-defense as the fixed reference. Adversaries include random-pool sampling, sequential near-duplicate expansion, region-diversity/query-filtering, paraphrasing and low-and-slow/session-split querying; all paid attempts count. Budgets500 are matched, and resource/time controls are disclosed.

Reject the proposed prevention benefit if it fails to reduce Student fidelity by at least5 points versus the best matched simple comparator across the predefined seeds, with a paired uncertainty interval excluding zero required for a positive claim; reject utility preservation if legitimate correct responses per all attempts fall by >2 points, coverage by >2 points, or an agreed latency limit is exceeded. These are proposed review thresholds, not measured effects or guaranteed power; the data plan explains larger sample needs. A successful detector alone does not establish H2. CPU clustering/counters are feasible in principle, but do not start implementation until H1/data/Teacher/baseline gates and prior comparison pass.

## Shared future evaluation

Primary prevention endpoint: Student free-generation agreement with a **fixed undefended Teacher reference** on locked held-out inputs. Also Student ground-truth accuracy/macro-F1, per-class effects, invalid rate and majority/pretrained/shuffled controls. Defended-Teacher task accuracy, correct-response rate over all legitimate attempts, answered-query conditional accuracy, coverage, refusal rate, p50/p95 latency, query/token/compute costs and peak memory must all be shown. Agreement loss against changed reference answers alone is not protection. Train fresh Students with matched settings and seeds17,23,42; report paired differences and seed variability. Separate delayed extraction from degradation at fixed attempted query budgets and record successful labels.

For detection, split benign reference, benign-only calibration and held-out benign sessions. No threshold tuning on attack/final data. Report FPR with uncertainty, TPR, ROC-AUC descriptively, alarm delay in attempted queries, time-to-alarm censored at budget, missed attacks, undecided sparse windows, mixed fractions0%,10%,25%,50%,100%, and heavy legitimate repeated-task traffic. Preserve natural chronology and identity for session tests; independently constructed shuffled windows are synthetic traffic stress tests, not real-user sessions. Test query-only and output-aware assumptions separately. Record source, length and intent composition, plus a matched benign/attacker-input negative control where no separation should be expected. Do not turn false positives from source shift into evidence of malicious intent.
